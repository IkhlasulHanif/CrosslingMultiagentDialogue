### Turn 0

Both paramagnetism and ferromagnetism require the presence of unpaired electrons. In paramagnetic materials, these unpaired electrons cause the material to be attracted to a magnetic field. In ferromagnetic materials, the unpaired electrons not only cause attraction to a magnetic field but also result in a permanent magnetic moment due to the alignment of their magnetic moments in the same direction. Therefore, the presence of unpaired electrons is a fundamental requirement for both these types of magnetism.
