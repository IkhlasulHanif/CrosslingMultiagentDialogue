The correct answer is B. Unpaired electrons.

In paramagnetic materials, these unpaired electrons contribute to a weak magnetic moment that aligns with an external magnetic field, while in ferromagnetic materials, unpaired electrons lead to a strong magnetic moment and a permanent magnetization even in the absence of an external field.
