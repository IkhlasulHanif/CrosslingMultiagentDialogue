## Stimulus

Question:
Some of Mars'water is frozen in the soil and the ice caps and some was lost due to solar wind stripping but much of the original water is thought to have been lost through another mechanism. What happened to this "lost"water?

Options:
A. it was vaporized during a period of intense volcanism and was lost to space through thermal escape
B. it was consumed by a civilization of thirsty Martians
C. it was broken into hydrogen and oxygen by ultraviolet light and the hydrogen was lost through thermal escape
D. it was stripped away by the magnetic field when it rapidly decreased in strength

## Target Turns

### Turn 0

The most widely accepted explanation for the "lost" water on Mars is that it was broken into hydrogen and oxygen by ultraviolet light, and the hydrogen was then lost to space through thermal escape. This process of photodissociation, where water molecules are broken down by solar ultraviolet radiation, results in the lighter hydrogen atoms escaping Mars' atmosphere due to their low mass and the weak gravitational pull of Mars. This theory is consistent with the current understanding of Mars' atmospheric loss over geological timescales.
