A. Fasciculations are never seen - This is incorrect. Fasciculations, or muscle twitches, are often seen in lower motor neuron lesions.

B. It leads to increased tone in the arm (hypertonia) - This is incorrect. Lower motor neuron lesions typically cause decreased muscle tone (hypotonia), not increased tone.

C. The reflexes are brisk - This is incorrect. Lower motor neuron lesions usually cause diminished or absent reflexes, not brisk reflexes.

D. An ulna nerve palsy is an example of a lower motor neurone lesion - This is correct. Ulnar nerve palsy is an example of a lower motor neuron lesion because it involves damage to the peripheral nervous system, affecting the motor neurons outside the central nervous system.

Therefore, the correct answer is D. An ulna nerve palsy is an example of a lower motor neurone lesion.
