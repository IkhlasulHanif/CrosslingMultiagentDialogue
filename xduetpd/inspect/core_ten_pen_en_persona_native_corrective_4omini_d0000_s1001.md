To evaluate the statements:

Statement 1: "A factor group of a non-Abelian group is non-Abelian."
This statement is false. A factor group (or quotient group) can be Abelian even if the original group is non-Abelian. For example, consider the symmetric group S3, which is non-Abelian. The quotient group S3/A3 (where A3 is the alternating group) is Abelian.

Statement 2: "If K is a normal subgroup of H and H is a normal subgroup of G, then K is a normal subgroup of G."
This statement is true. If K is normal in H and H is normal in G, then for every g in G and k in K, gkg^{-1} is in H (since K is normal in H) and thus in K (since K is normal in H). Therefore, K is normal in G.

So the correct evaluation of the statements is:
Statement 1: False
Statement 2: True
