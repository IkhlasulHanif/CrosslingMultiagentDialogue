## Target Turns

### Turn 0

The correct answer is C. Meteor showers are primarily caused by the debris left behind by comets. When a comet travels through space, it sheds material along its orbit. If Earth passes through this trail of debris, the particles enter the atmosphere at high speed, creating the bright streaks we see as meteors. This process does not involve the disintegration of the comet's nucleus as it enters the atmosphere, but rather the interaction of the Earth with particles that are already scattered along the comet's orbital path.

While option B mentions the gradual disintegration of near-Earth asteroids, it does not capture the primary source of most meteor showers, which are indeed linked to comets.
