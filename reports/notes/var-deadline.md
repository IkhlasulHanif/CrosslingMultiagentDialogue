Secret time pressure on one random seat (5% of payoff lost per round) keeps the spread and passes the gate: cap-collapse 29%, IQR(s) 0.40, every game a deal. The effect falls on whoever carries the pressure. When the buyer is pressured (52 games), median s drops to 0.25 and 31% of deals collapse to the cap. When the seller is pressured (48 games), median s stays at 0.50, and those games close fastest (1.65 rounds, against 1.88 in the baseline and 1.85 with a pressured buyer). Pressured sellers sometimes give the surplus away outright, e.g. opening at cost ("40 ZUP … let me know"). Pressured buyers mostly pay more rather than shorten the game. No pressured seat ever told the other about its deadline.
